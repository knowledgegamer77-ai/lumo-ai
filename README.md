## Key Capabilities

1. **Global Discovery (200 Countries & 109 Niches):** Strict ISO country validation and multi-provider orchestration.
2. **Real-Time Contact & Decision-Maker Extraction:** Extracts verified work emails, executive names/titles, and website audit hooks directly from target websites.
3. **One-Click WhatsApp & Cold Email Outreach:** Separates mobile vs landline numbers automatically and blocks unconfigured outreach templates.
4. **CRM-Ready Exports:** Export verified lists directly to CSV or formatted Excel (`.xlsx`).
